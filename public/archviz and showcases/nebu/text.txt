Client : Gedico Developments
 
Nebu, is an ancient Egyptian term that means gold. It symbolizes wealth, eternity, and divine power. Gold was highly significant in Egyptian culture, often associated with the gods and the afterlife due to its incorruptibility and everlasting shine. The hieroglyph for Nebu consists of a necklace with beads, representing the precious metal’s cultural and spiritual value. In modern contexts, ” Nebu ” can evoke ideas of luxury, heritage, and timelessness, making it a powerful name for projects or brands connected to these themes
