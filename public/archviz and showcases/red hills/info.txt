
 
Red Hills
Location : Egypt, Sahl Hashish

Design Date : 2025

 
Red Hills by Enza Developments is an exclusive resort-style real estate project located in the heart of Sahl Hasheesh on Egypt’s Red Sea coast. Designed with a modern architectural vision and a focus on luxury living, the development offers a harmonious blend of contemporary design and natural coastal beauty.

The project features a variety of unit types, including studios, apartments, penthouses, and chalets, all delivered with high-quality finishing and thoughtful layouts that maximize space, light, and comfort. Each residence is crafted to provide an elevated lifestyle experience, appealing to both end-users and investors.

Residents and visitors enjoy a comprehensive range of resort-level amenities, including multiple swimming pools, landscaped gardens, sports facilities, and dedicated leisure spaces. With 24/7 security, maintenance services, and smart living systems, Red Hills ensures convenience and peace of mind for all occupants.

Strategically positioned within minutes of pristine sandy beaches and just a short drive from Hurghada International Airport, Red Hills combines accessibility with the serenity of seaside living. This prime location enhances its appeal for vacation homes as well as long-term residential and rental investments.

With flexible payment plans and strong potential for capital appreciation, Red Hills by Enza Developments represents a compelling opportunity for those seeking a premium lifestyle destination or a strategic investment on the Red Sea.
